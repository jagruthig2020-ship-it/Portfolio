01 / THE REASON

Why we built it.

We’re exploring a small companion robot for elderly care: something that could come closer, speak a reminder, and help a caregiver know when something might be wrong. MILO is a group project, and we’re still working toward a dependable prototype.

02 / THE BUILD

How it works.

The proposed system combines a mobile base, voice interaction, reminders, and wearable safety sensing. Navigation, caregiver alerts, and person-aware behaviour are part of the design direction.

03 / RIGHT NOW

Where it stands.

MILO is a group project in development. The complete care workflow is not implemented yet; the robot’s voice, navigation, wearable, and alert functions still need to be brought together and tested.
