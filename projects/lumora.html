01 / THE REASON

Why I built it.

I didn’t want another page about Harry Potter. I wanted a room I could do things in: light it up, lift an object, try a spell and watch it respond. Lumora is where I’ve been trying to turn that idea into something you can explore.

02 / THE BUILD

How it works.

I’m building a room around spell-driven interactions: Lumos and Nox for the light, and spells that let you lift objects and discover things in the scene. I’m still working on how those interactions should feel together.
