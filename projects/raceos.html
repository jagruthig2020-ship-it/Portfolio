01 / THE REASON

Why we built it.

We wanted to look more closely at the pit-stop decision in a race. RaceOS takes lap number, tyre age, tyre compound, and position, then uses a model trained on race data to explore a strategy prediction. It’s an experiment we can test, rather than a strategy we can promise will win.

02 / THE BUILD

How it works.

FastF1 supplies the race data. We prepare the features, train tabular models, and use Streamlit to explore the strategy outputs. Cleaning and rebalancing the data were a significant part of making the results useful.

03 / RIGHT NOW

Where it stands.

The prediction pipeline is a working experiment. Its output depends on the races and assumptions in the dataset; it does not cover every factor a live race strategy team uses.
