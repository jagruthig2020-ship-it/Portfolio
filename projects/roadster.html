01 / THE REASON

Why we’re building it.

We’ve been talking about a Tesla Roadster-inspired car demo, and we want to take it further as our major project. I want us to reach the point where we can put a car on the floor and show what we actually made it do.

02 / THE BUILD

What we’re planning.

We’re starting with the vehicle: the chassis, drive, steering, power, and control. Those parts need to work together before we add anything more ambitious. The point is to test one behaviour at a time, then build on it.

03 / RIGHT NOW

Where it stands.

This is a planned group project. We’re still deciding the final scope and hardware, so there isn’t a completed car demo to show yet. I’ll add build photos, tests, and the demo as we reach those stages.
