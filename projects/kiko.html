01 / THE REASON

Why I built it.

I saw people around me get stuck on computer tasks I barely thought about. A familiar screen to me could be a confusing one to them. I built KIKO because I wanted to help with that exact moment: what do I do next?

02 / THE BUILD

How it works.

The desktop prototype combines step-by-step guidance, practice tasks, a learner profile, and multilingual interaction. It uses a local Python setup and explores local language-model assistance through Ollama.

03 / RIGHT NOW

Where it stands.

The prototype runs locally. Telugu guidance, voice input, and local-model responses still have inconsistencies. I’m working on making the guidance dependable before expanding the feature list.
