01 / THE REASON

Why I built it.

I work late a lot, but being awake and being able to focus aren’t always the same thing. I wanted to understand why some nights felt easier than others. ChronoMind AI started with that question, and with testing whether the data could tell me anything useful.

02 / THE BUILD

How it works.

I built cognitive tasks around reaction time, visual search, and working memory, then compared personal baselines with machine-learning models. The aim is to use measured performance to explore a more useful schedule.

03 / RIGHT NOW

Where it stands.

The tasks and modelling pipeline are experimental. A personal baseline has been difficult to beat; I’m refining how the data is collected and evaluated before treating the schedule suggestions as reliable.
